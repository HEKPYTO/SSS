reuters_apte.arff.gz — the reuters dataset of the paper's experiments
======================================================================

This is a derived representation of the Reuters-21578 text categorization
test collection, Distribution 1.0, "Apte" (ModApte) split, compiled by
David D. Lewis. Current location of the original collection:

    https://www.daviddlewis.com/resources/testcollections/reuters21578/

    the name "Reuters-21578, Distribution 1.0", and inform readers of
    its current location.

THIS RESTRICTION IS NARROWER THAN THE LICENSES OF THE OTHER ACCOMPANYING
FILES: this data file is for research use only.

Modification: the collection is converted to a sparse ARFF term-frequency
matrix — 8941 documents, 10105 numeric term features, 33 categories. The
representation is ANONYMIZED: attributes are named a1..a10105 and classes
cls0..cls32, so neither the vocabulary nor the category labels are part
of the file and no article text can be reconstructed from it.
